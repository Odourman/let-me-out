# Negative trials

All captures here are developer observations in Gopher2600. The probe binaries deliberately force one kernel over a controlled raster band. Their position inputs and old-mask oracles come from the frozen ROM fixtures. These builds are rejected and must not be treated as playable checkpoints.

- **BALL+M0, separated X.** Subdivide the key silhouette between a two-clock ball and a two-clock missile. Park both objects before the visible band and change COLUPF and COLUP0 independently on the selected rows. Result at P0 X24/Y151 and key X85/Y153: 0/14 key pixels, 20 extra gold pixels, 3 missing and 21 extra yellow pixels, SM 18/20, 267 scanlines. The extra gold actually appears at X156–159 on raster lines 169, 171, 173, 175, and 177 in the native 160×214 capture. The writes held the gold PF color through the right wall and missed the key window. This differs from the historical one-ball trial (8/14 plus 12 extra).
- **Independent M1 for part of F, five Y86.** Use the four-clock missile as an additional white span with a single parked M1 and without additional P1 resets. For X20,44,68,92,116, observed M0/M1/F/SM/SF are 0/28, 0/28, 32/38, 0/20, 22/22, with 10 extra white and 2 extra yellow pixels at 262 scanlines. F has noncontiguous row bits and varying spans that the anchored missile cannot exactly reproduce. This fixed probe covers one position and does not establish a limit for every possible dynamic P0/P1/M1 kernel.
- **Wall bridge selection, rejected intermediate builds.** The first X60–62 build shortened the buffer used for blank P1 and produced 13 extra white pixels on the BOTTOM top border. Restoring the blank buffer removed them. The next version shifted an existing four-pixel PF gap between rows in 30 standard frames. A full search for a preferred regular-phase slot then ran past the VBLANK timer in the five-NPC door fixture. Gopher measured the first bridge setup at SL258 with corrupt frame lengths. This failure persisted after moving the final RTS away from the F6 hotspot. The delivered guard tests the first queue slot without a second search.
